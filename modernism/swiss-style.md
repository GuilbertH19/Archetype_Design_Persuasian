## What is it?

Swiss Style, also called the International Typographic Style, is a modernist graphic design style that developed in Switzerland during the 1950s. It emphasizes clarity, order, readability, and visual communication. Designers commonly used grid-based layouts, sans-serif typography, photography, and limited decorative elements.

## How do you recognize or use it?

Swiss Style can be recognized by its organized grid structure, clean typography, strong alignment, and clear hierarchy of information. Sans-serif typefaces, asymmetrical layouts, geometric forms, and photography are commonly used.

Designers use this style when information needs to be communicated clearly and efficiently. It is especially useful for posters, publications, signage, corporate identities, and other forms of visual communication.
